Venue & travel

3DOR 2027 is hosted at the Museum of the History of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, in the heart of Plaka.

Venue

Built
1837-1841
Architects
Kleanthis & Schaubert
Museum since
1987

3DOR 2027 is hosted at the Museum of the History of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (ΜΙΠΑ), in the building that housed Greece's first university from 1837 to 1841. In the heart of Athens, right below the Acropolis, the building was originally the home and architectural office of Stamatios Kleanthis and Eduard Schaubert, the architects behind the first urban plans for the newly founded capital. It became a museum in 1987.

Getting to Athens

Athens International Airport “Eleftherios Venizelos” (ATH) is the main gateway, connected to the city centre by metro line 3, suburban rail and express buses. Within the city, three metro lines plus trams and buses cover most districts.

Early September in Athens is typically warm and dry, with daytime temperatures often around 30 °C.
