Venue & travel · 2-3 Sep 2027

The Old University

A neoclassical house in the heart of Plaka that was Greece's first university, now the venue for 3DOR 2027.

Address
Tholou 5, Plaka, 105 56 Athens
Nearest metro
Monastiraki & Syntagma ≈10-15 min walk
First university
1837-1841

History

  1. Before 1800

    An Ottoman-period house stood on the site, part of the old neighbourhood of Plaka beneath the Acropolis.

  2. 1831

    Architects Stamatios Kleanthis and Eduard Schaubert bought the house and restored it as their own residence and architectural office.

  3. 1837-1841

    The building became the first seat of the University of Athens, home to the newly founded Greek state's first university.

  4. Later 19th c.

    After the university outgrew the building, it served a series of other uses, including as army barracks.

  5. 1967

    The building passed into the ownership of the University of Athens.

  6. 1987

    It opened to the public as the Museum of the History of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens.

  7. 2027

    The museum hosts the 20th 3DOR Symposium.

Getting there

Monastiraki (lines 1 & 3) is about a 10 minute walk through the Plaka lanes.

Syntagma (lines 2 & 3, and the airport line) is about a 12 minute walk.

Akropoli (line 2) is about a 15 minute walk, mostly through Plaka and past the Acropolis.

Open in Google Maps

From Athens International Airport "Eleftherios Venizelos" (ATH), metro line 3 runs directly to Syntagma and on to Monastiraki; the X95 express bus also runs to Syntagma.

Early September in Athens is typically warm and dry, with daytime temperatures often around 30 °C.

Collections

Scientific instruments

19th-century instruments used for teaching and research across the university's early schools.

Seals, 1837

The original seals of the university and its first four schools, struck in the year of its founding.

Rare books

Early editions and textbooks from the university's first decades.

Manuscripts

Administrative records and correspondence documenting the university's founding and early years.

Portraits

Portraits of professors and rectors from the university's first generations.
