Call for papers

20 Years EG 3DOR

1 March 2027 Full paper submission deadline

See the important dates page for the full review timeline.

The Eurographics Symposium on 3D Object Retrieval (EG 3DOR) is a classic venue for researchers and practitioners working on all aspects of 3D shape: search and retrieval, 3D data management, 3D vision, geometry processing and machine learning.

In 2027 we are celebrating 20 consecutive 3DOR Symposia (since 2008).

Topics of interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • 3D shape representations
  • 3D shape semantics
  • Dynamic 3D shapes and scenes
  • 3D mesh segmentation
  • Multimodal descriptors
  • Geometry vs appearance
  • Partial, part-in-whole, and many-to-many matching
  • Matching under uncertainty and noise
  • Similarity of non-rigid shapes
  • Query interfaces and search modalities
  • Sketch-based retrieval
  • Vision-language modelling in 3D search
  • Benchmarking, datasets, evaluation metrics
  • Applications in CAD, architecture, games, robotics, biometrics, medicine, biology and cultural heritage

State-of-the-art papers are particularly encouraged.
