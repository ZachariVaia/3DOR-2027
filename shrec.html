SHREC 2027

The SHREC evaluation tracks are presented at the 3DOR symposium. Track proposals for 2027 are due on 27 November 2026.

27 November 2026 Track proposal deadline

Notification, track running period and results submission dates follow. See important dates.

What a track is

A SHREC track is a comparative evaluation on a shared benchmark. The track organisers define a task, prepare a dataset and ground truth, collect runs from participants, and report the combined results at the symposium.

Tracks cover retrieval as well as neighbouring problems on 3D data such as classification, correspondence and segmentation.

Proposing a track

Detailed proposal requirements, the submission channel and the full track calendar for 2027 will be published on this page. If you are considering organising a track, it helps to get in touch early.
