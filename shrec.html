SHREC 2027

The SHREC evaluation tracks are presented at the 3DOR symposium. Proposals for 2027 tracks are due on 27 November 2026.

27 November 2026 Track proposal deadline

Deadline for submitting a SHREC 2027 track proposal. Notification, track running period and results submission dates will follow.

Proposing a track

A SHREC track is a comparative evaluation on a shared benchmark: the organisers define a task, prepare a dataset and ground truth, collect runs from participants and report the results at the symposium. Tracks cover retrieval as well as related problems such as classification, correspondence and segmentation of 3D data.

Detailed proposal requirements, the submission channel and the full track calendar for 2027 will be published on this page. If you are considering organising a track, get in touch with the organisers early.
