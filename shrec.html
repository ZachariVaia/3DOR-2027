SHREC 2027 - 3D Shape Retrieval Challenge 2027

About SHREC

The general objective of the 3D Shape Retrieval Challenge (SHREC) is to evaluate the effectiveness of 3D shape retrieval algorithms. SHREC has run annually since 2006, and SHREC 2027 is organised alongside the Eurographics Symposium on 3D Object Retrieval, where results are presented.

A SHREC track is a comparative evaluation on a shared benchmark: the track organisers define a task, prepare a dataset and ground truth, collect runs from participants, and report the combined results at the symposium. Past editions have covered rigid and non-rigid models, partial and complete models, sketch-based retrieval, and domain-specific applications such as CAD, biometrics and molecular shapes, alongside topics like metric learning, correspondence, registration and classification.

Proposing a track

Track proposals go to the SHREC chair, Remco Veltkamp (r.c.veltkamp@uu.nl). At least one author of each accepted track must register for the symposium and present the results. Full track papers are reviewed for publication in Computers & Graphics; shorter contributions are published in the Eurographics Digital Library.
