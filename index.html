3DOR is the annual forum for research on the retrieval, matching and analysis of 3D objects and shapes. The 2027 edition will be held in Athens over two days of paper presentations and SHREC track results.

Scope

The International Symposium on 3D Object Retrieval is a leading venue for researchers and practitioners working on 3D data management, search and retrieval. It brings together state of the art methods, novel applications and theoretical advances across geometry, learning and perception.

The symposium runs as a single track over two days, so every paper and every SHREC track is presented to the whole audience.

Topics of interest

  • Multimodal and learning based retrieval
  • Generative models for 3D shapes and retrieval
  • Zero shot, few shot and category agnostic methods
  • 3D shape representation and similarity
  • Geometry and topology based matching
  • Part based and structural matching
  • Non rigid and articulated shape analysis
  • Interactive search interfaces and user studies
  • Applications in AR and VR, medicine, cultural heritage and CAD
  • Large scale 3D data management
  • Benchmarking, datasets and evaluation

The list above reflects the scope of recent editions and is indicative. The topics for 2027, together with any specific theme, are confirmed with the call for papers.

SHREC 2027

The SHREC tracks are presented at the symposium. The Shape Retrieval Challenge encourages the community to build shared datasets and benchmarks for evaluating retrieval methods, and has grown over the years to cover classification, correspondence and segmentation of 3D data as well.

Each track is proposed and run by its own organisers, who define the task, prepare the data, collect the submitted runs and report the comparison at the symposium.

Proposing a track? Proposals are due on 27 November 2026, well before the paper deadline. See the SHREC 2027 page.

Replicability

Authors are encouraged to make their code and data available so that results can be reproduced, and to apply for a replicability stamp. Whether the stamp is part of the 2027 review process is confirmed with the call for papers.
