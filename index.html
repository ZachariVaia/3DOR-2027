Symposium on 3D Object Retrieval

3DOR 2027

Athens, Greece

2-3 September 2027 · Full paper deadline 1 March 2027

3DOR is the annual meeting point for research on the retrieval, matching and analysis of 3D objects and shapes. The 2027 edition comes to Athens for two days of paper presentations and SHREC track results.

The website is being built up as details are confirmed: the full call for papers, committees and programme will follow in the coming months.

About the symposium

Papers

Work on 3D shape retrieval, matching, representation and understanding is presented over two days of single-track sessions.

SHREC tracks

The SHREC contest tracks report comparative evaluations on shared benchmarks, with results presented at the symposium.

Athens

The 2027 edition is hosted in Athens in early September. Venue and travel details will be published here.

Proposing a SHREC track? Proposals are due on 27 November 2026, earlier than the paper deadline. See the SHREC 2027 page.
