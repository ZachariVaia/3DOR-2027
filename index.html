The Eurographics Symposium on 3D Object Retrieval (EG 3DOR) is a classic venue for researchers and practitioners working on all aspects of 3D shape: search and retrieval, 3D data management, 3D vision, geometry processing and machine learning.

In 2027 we are celebrating 20 consecutive 3DOR Symposia (since 2008).

See the full list of topics of interest in the call for papers.

SHREC 2027

The SHREC tracks are presented at the symposium. The Shape Retrieval Challenge encourages the community to build shared datasets and benchmarks for evaluating retrieval methods, and has grown over the years to cover classification, correspondence and segmentation of 3D data as well.

Each track is proposed and run by its own organisers, who define the task, prepare the data, collect the submitted runs and report the comparison at the symposium.

Proposing a track? Proposals are due on 27 November 2026, well before the paper deadline. See the SHREC 2027 page.

Replicability

Authors are encouraged to make their code and data available so that results can be reproduced, and to apply for a replicability stamp. Whether the stamp is part of the 2027 review process is confirmed with the call for papers.
